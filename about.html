Alex "Nacho" de Brantes

Maker, photographer, builder

Alex de Brantes is an entrepreneur with a passion for photography and videography from Arkansas. Social media gave microphones to many, but it put a camera in my hands.

My interest in photography was born out of necessity. I've had multiple ecommerce businesses over the years; on a shoestring budget you learn to wear a lot of hats. The most important one being product photography.

Instagram's rise in popularity, and the boom of the influencer, meant my brands needed to deliver top-notch product photography for the grid or be left behind. The pressure to deliver quality content spurred my love for photo and video.

Today he leads Homesteader Labs, the logbook of a half acre: every build named, measured and shipped as a post, a clip and a printable part.

Dawn de Brantes · TheHippieDiva

Marketer, music-video maker, podcast host

Dawn de Brantes is TheHippieDiva: a serial entrepreneur and marketer with 25+ years in startups, decentralized sales and software. After four and a half years in federal prison, she came home and rebuilt in public.

She writes and directs AI music videos (One Mirror at a Time, AI N00b), co-hosts the podcast Two Women One Prison with Sabrena Morgan, and wrote Commissary B*tch in the Kitch, a cookbook for women in federal prison. She teaches people how to make AI work for their lives and set their creativity free.

Co-host of Two Women One Prison.
